## Night Checklist

1. Supply each army (within 2 hexes of a source, or pay 1 WC, or weary +1)
2. Rest: supplied armies that didn't move get weary −1
3. Sieges: nightly Defense check, Strain +1 per besieged settlement
4. 3+ Contested hexes → Strain +1
5. ★ Pay Day and war fatigue
6. War Clock +1
7. Check Strain crises, then Clock escalations

## Reckoning (end of war)

- [ ] Contested hexes: Defense/Exploration check each; failures are lost
- [ ] Enemy-held hexes stay lost unless the terms return them
- [ ] Leftover WC → Commodities (1:1)
- [ ] Strain ≥ 10: +1d4 Unrest; ≥ 15: also +1 Ruin
- [ ] Apply banked Kingdom XP (+ the campaign award)
- [ ] Apply the outcome (Victory / Terms / Truce / Collapse)
- [ ] Resume Kingdom turns

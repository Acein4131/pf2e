# Days of War — Daily-Turn Warfare for Kingmaker (PF2e)

A homebrew replacement for running the war against **Fort Drelev** (Kingmaker
Chapter 7, *Blood for Blood*) through Kingdom turns. Each **day is a turn**. The
party hexplores, adventures, and commands as they normally would, while armies
march, raid, besiege, and clash across the map. The kingdom's **statistics** are
used for every check, and the kingdom can take real losses: Unrest, Ruin, lost
hexes, and destroyed structures. It **never uses the Kingdom turn procedure**.
No Resource Points, no Kingdom-turn activities, no monthly upkeep.

## Why

Read as written, the module has the PCs defend Tatzlford, maybe fight one more
army, and then walk into Fort Drelev, with any larger campaign squeezed into
monthly Kingdom turns. That pacing is wrong for a war that should feel like a
month of destruction. A month-long abstraction turns weeks of marching, raiding,
and burning villages into a single downtime roll. Days of War compresses the
same conflict into roughly 15–30 played days. Every decision is made on the map:
whether the heroes ride out to kill the enemy captain or stay with the levies,
whether to save the bridge or the farms, whether to storm the fort now or
starve it first.

## What it borrows from the book

All of this lives in the Kingmaker Adventure Path hardcover and is used unchanged:

- **Kingdom skills, Control DC, Unrest and Ruin penalties, Fame/Infamy** (Appendix 2).
- **Army stat blocks, conditions, tactics, gear, the Recover/Train/Outfit/Recruit
  activities, fortifications, and War encounters** (Appendix 3, pp. 566–583).
- **Traveling with an Army** during hexploration (Appendix 3). The book already
  lets armies move alongside the PCs; this system extends that so armies the
  PCs *aren't* with can still act every day.
- **Liberation Points** for Fort Drelev (Chapter 7, pp. 301–302).

## What it replaces

- The Deploy Army / Garrison Army / Offensive Gambit cadence of once per Kingdom turn.
- The "wartime" Unrest rule (1d6 Unrest and +1 Ruin per Kingdom turn with
  warfare, Appendix 3 p. 566) and Chapter 8's Wartime Fatigue. **Home Front
  Strain** (see `rules.md`) takes their place for the duration of the war.

Foundry integration is out of scope for now. These documents are designed to be
run at the table with the Kingmaker kingdom sheet and a hex map.

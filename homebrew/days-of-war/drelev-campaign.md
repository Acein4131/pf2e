# The Drelev Campaign — A Days of War Kit

*For Kingmaker Chapter 7, "Blood for Blood." PCs level 10–11, kingdom level about 10.*

Baron Hannis Drelev has declared war. Rather than wait for Pitax or the Tiger
Lords to do his work, the craven baron spends Irovetti's silver on mercenaries,
borrows hill giants and Tiger Lord riders, and sets out to bleed the PCs' western
border before they can march on him. This kit turns that declaration into a
**campaign of roughly 15–30 days**, played with the rules in [`rules.md`](rules.md).
It ends with Fort Drelev liberated (or not), feeding straight into the module's
Liberation Points and the fight in Drelev Keep.

Page numbers refer to the Kingmaker Adventure Path hardcover.

---

## 1. Where This Fits

- **After the Battle for Tatzlford** (Part 1, pp. 288–299). Kisandra Numesti's plea
  (p. 299) and Drelev's open hostility make this the natural point to declare war.
- **Replaces** the gap between Part 1 and Part 2, which the module expects you to fill
  with Kingdom turns. **Part 2** (Fort Drelev and Drelev Keep, pp. 300–317) becomes the
  campaign's climax rather than its opening.
- **Leave Part 3** (Armag's Tomb) **after the war.** Armag is busy delving his
  ancestor's tomb and only lends Drelev riders. The campaign seeds Part 3 (Windchaser,
  the Tiger Lord hills, Tamary Numesti) without resolving it.

## 2. The Theater

| Zone / Site | Role in the war |
| --- | --- |
| **Tatzlford** (GB16) | Your western anchor and Drelev's first target. |
| **Narlmarches** (your western hexes) | Your Friendly hexes, farms, and work sites: what Drelev's raiders want to burn. |
| **Hooktongue Slough** (zone 10, pp. 94–103) | A greater-difficult swamp between the two realms. It's slow and dangerous for armies without guides. Home to M'botuu's boggards (HT6) and the bog striders of Tok-Nikrat (HT12). |
| **Wyvernstone Bridge** (HT1) | The only good crossing of the East Sellen before it empties into the Slough. **Drelev holds it at the start.** |
| **Drelev plains** (zone 11, pp. 103–105) | Open grassland west and north of the Slough. Fast ground for cavalry. Contains Speartooth's Den (DR1), the Wild Horses (DR3), and the Desperate Refugees (DR4). |
| **Drelev's Forward Camp** (new) | Place it on the plains **2–3 hexes from Fort Drelev, toward your kingdom**. It's Drelev's supply depot for the field armies. |
| **Fort Drelev** (DR2) | The baron's seat on Lake Hooktongue (pp. 300–317). |
| **Tiger Lord hills** (zone 12) | West of Drelev. The Outriders ride in from here. |

> **Check your map.** The campaign assumes the East Sellen and Hooktongue Slough lie
> between your kingdom and Fort Drelev, as they do for kingdoms built out of the
> Greenbelt and Narlmarches. If your map differs, keep the idea that the bridge is the
> fast way across a barrier, and move it to fit.

## 3. Setup (Day 0)

1. **Record the kingdom:** level, Control DC, skill modifiers, Unrest, Ruins,
   Fame/Infamy, stored Commodities, settlements, and armies.
2. **Mobilization check** → starting War Chest (`rules.md` §3).
3. **Starting Strain** = current Unrest ÷ 2 (rounded down).
4. **Your armies:** place any armies the kingdom already has. If the PCs kept the
   **Tatzlford Town Guard** (Army 7) and **Narlmarch Hunters** (Army 6) after Part 1
   (p. 296), they are in Tatzlford. Those two armies don't scale with kingdom level
   unless the PCs paid to keep them on, as the book describes.
5. **Enemy armies:** place them as shown in §4.
6. **War Clock** = 0, adjusted by how Tatzlford went:

| Tatzlford outcome | Adjustments |
| --- | --- |
| Successful Victory, Ameon Trask **captured** | Clock 0. If Ameon is made helpful (p. 299), he gives up the whole enemy order of battle: on Day 1 the GM reveals every enemy army's location. |
| Successful Victory, Ameon **killed** | Clock 0. |
| Victory, Ameon **escaped** | Clock 2. Ameon commands the Pitaxian Free Company (see §4). |
| Costly Victory | Clock 2, Strain +1. |
| Total Defeat | Clock 4, Strain +3. Tatzlford starts **enemy-held**, garrisoned by any surviving Drelev Irregulars and Troll Marauders (p. 297). Retaking it comes first. |

### Drelev's supply

Enemy armies draw supply from **Fort Drelev**, the **Forward Camp**, and any captured
friendly settlement. Mercenary skirmishers with Live off the Land are always supplied.
Unsupplied enemy armies increase weary each Night, the same as yours.

The **Forward Camp** is a depot (Sabotage DC 28). Destroying it (Mission 2 or
Sabotage critical success) gives War Clock −2 and +3 WC from captured stores.

### Drelev's Posture

At Dusk, move enemy armies according to the current Posture.

| Posture | When | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Raiding** | Clock 0–7 | The Raiders and Outriders raid. Everyone else holds. |
| **Offensive** | Clock 8–15 | The Stonethrowers and Free Company (plus the Siege Battery once it arrives) march on the **nearest friendly settlement**, usually Tatzlford, and Besiege it. The raiders keep raiding. |
| **Total War** | Clock 16+ | Every field army seeks out the nearest friendly army or settlement and attacks. The Fort Guard always holds the fort. |
| **Entrenched** (override) | **3 or more** Drelev armies destroyed, **or** a friendly army with siege engines within 2 hexes of Fort Drelev | Drelev panics. Every surviving army marches back to Fort Drelev and garrisons. Raiding stops. The War Clock still advances. |

## 5. The War Clock: Drelev's Escalations

Each escalation triggers the first time the Clock reaches its value. Announce it at
Dawn through the fiction: a messenger, smoke, a rumor, a corpse on a gibbet.

| Clock | Escalation | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **4** | *Iron Curfew* | Drelev tightens his grip on the town. Lose 1 banked LP. When Part 2's infiltration begins, every infiltration DC (p. 303) is +1. |
| **6** | *The Pay Convoy* | A Pitaxian wagon train of silver sets out to pay the mercenaries. **Mission 7** becomes available. |
| **8** | *A Price on Their Heads* | Posture → **Offensive**. Drelev posts a bounty on the PCs. On the next day they travel, they meet a band of bounty hunters (a Moderate 11 encounter of your choice). |
| **12** | *Pitax's Loan* | The **Pitax Siege Battery** arrives at Fort Drelev. If the PCs hold Wyvernstone Bridge, it arrives 3 days later instead. |
| **16** | *The Wall Is Finished* | Posture → **Total War**. Drelev finally closes the gap in Fort Drelev's wall (B1, p. 301). See §8: the wall now counts as a full Wooden Wall. |
| **20** | *Armag's Gift* | The **Tiger Lord Warband** rides in from the western map edge. |
| **24** | *Conscription* | Drelev drags townsfolk into the **Drelev Conscripts**, placed at Fort Drelev. Lose 2 banked LP, because the town's boldest are now in chains or in the levy. |
| **27** | *Blood in the Square* | Drelev hangs townsfolk suspected of helping the PCs. Lose 3 banked LP and Strain +2. |
| **30** | *Irovetti's Hand* | Pitax openly guarantees Drelev's throne. If the PCs aren't already besieging Fort Drelev, the war ends in an **Armed Truce** (§9). |

## 6. Missions

Missions are where the party's hexploration and encounters drive the war. Each is a
normal adventuring session. **(Hope)** marks missions that reduce Strain when
completed.

**Banked Liberation Points:** the campaign can bank **at most 10 LP** toward Fort
Drelev's 20 (p. 301). Whispers Behind the Walls can contribute **at most 6** of those.

### Mission 1 — The Wyvernstone Crossing (HT1) · Named Battle
The Pitaxian Free Company holds the bridge behind field works (trench: AC 15, HP 1).
- **Battle:** a full War encounter. The bridge is a narrow front: no more than **2
  armies per side** may be engaged at once. Run the commander as Strike the Commander.
- **PC strike:** before the battle, the party can hit the bridge's command post at
  night: the commander plus 4 Drelev guards (p. 304). Success means the Free Company
  starts the battle shaken 2 and not fortified.
- **Reward:** you hold the bridge. Your armies cross the East Sellen here without the
  river penalty, the Siege Battery escalation is delayed (§5), and War Clock −2.

### Mission 2 — Burn the Forward Camp (Drelev plains)
The Stonethrowers (and the Troll Marauders, if alive) guard Drelev's depot.
- **Infiltration:** DC 28 Stealth to approach (Avoid Notice). Inside, the party faces
  the giants' elite chieftain and a few hill giants (around Severe 11) while setting fires.
- **Battle option:** defeat the garrison armies, and the camp falls as well.
- **Reward:** the depot is destroyed, War Clock −2, and +3 WC. Enemy field armies more
  than 2 hexes from Fort Drelev become unsupplied.

### Mission 3 — The Strider's Son (HT6 M'botuu → HT12 Tok-Nikrat) · (Hope: Strain −1)
Rescue Ka-Kekt from M'botuu (Chapter 2, Part 9, p. 154) and return him to Tok-Nikrat.
- **Reward:** the bog striders become allies. Your armies treat Hooktongue Slough as
  **difficult** terrain, and you can Forage in swamp hexes. **Call Allies** can recruit
  the bog strider specialized army (Appendix 3) for **0 WC**.
- If M'botuu is **defeated or allied**, the Hooktongue Raiders lose *Swamp Guides*, and
  Daily Event 18 no longer applies.

### Mission 4 — Windchaser (DR3)
Make the awakened stallion friendly (p. 105).
- **Reward:** +2 circumstance bonus to Scout the Front in zones 11 and 12. Once during
  the war, Windchaser leads one army along hidden trails: it moves up to 3 open hexes in
  a single day and can't be intercepted by Patrols. If made helpful, Windchaser also
  reveals the Tiger Lords' hidden valley, a Part 3 lead.

### Mission 5 — The Desperate Refugees (DR4) · (Hope: Strain −2)
Escort the refugees (p. 105) to any friendly settlement. They move as infantry (1
activity per day) and the Hooktongue Raiders hunt them. If raiders end Dusk in their
hex without a friendly army there, the escort fails.
- **Reward:** Strain −2, Unrest −2 (as written, applied immediately), and +1 banked LP
  (their stories reach Fort Drelev). The free Trade Shop from the book is available
  when Kingdom turns resume.

### Mission 6 — Speartooth (DR1)
Hunt the legendary smilodon (p. 104).
- **Reward:** The Tiger Lords revere Speartooth, so its death is a dire omen. Every
  Tiger Lord army immediately attempts a DC 28 Morale check and **leaves the campaign**
  on a failure. The 2,000 gp bounty is paid as written.

### Mission 7 — The Pay Convoy (triggered at Clock 6)
A convoy of wagons and Pitaxian guards (a Severe 11 encounter) appears on the
southwestern map edge. It travels 1 hex per day toward Fort Drelev and arrives on the
4th day. Scouting it reveals its route.
- **Intercepted:** +4 WC, and every **Mercenary** army gains 1 Doubt.
- **Arrives:** every Mercenary army loses all Doubt, and the Fort Guard recovers 1 HP.

### Mission 8 — A Flower Through the Ring (Fort Drelev, B8)
With Kisandra's jade ring (p. 299), slip into Fort Drelev using the module's
infiltration rules (p. 303) and meet **Satinder Morne**.
- **Reward:** +1 banked LP. From now on, Whispers Behind the Walls gets a +2 circumstance
  bonus and never takes the courier penalty. Satinder reveals the **secret escape tunnel**
  (B5, p. 301) for the final assault (§8).

### Mission 9 — Ameon's Last Contract (only if Ameon escaped Tatzlford)
Hunt Ameon Trask in the field. He moves with the Free Company and goes to ground in its
camp at night.
- **Reward:** if he is killed or captured, the Free Company becomes shaken 1 and
  gains 1 Doubt. A captured Ameon can be turned as described on p. 299.

### Mission 10 — Retake Tatzlford (only after a Total Defeat)
As p. 299: storm the town or sneak in and remove Ameon (or his lieutenant), which makes
the occupying troops panic and disband.
- **Reward:** Tatzlford is Friendly again and Strain −2.

## 8. The Fall of Fort Drelev

The war is won when **Baron Drelev is defeated and the PCs reach 20 Liberation
Points**, exactly as Part 2 requires (p. 301). The campaign decides **how many LP the
PCs bring in** and **who is still defending the town**.

### Liberation Points from the campaign

At the start of the final approach, add up the banked LP (maximum 10):

- Missions 5 and 8, and Whispers Behind the Walls (maximum 6 from Whispers).
- **+1 LP for each Drelev army destroyed** during the campaign (maximum +4). Every
  dead mercenary is one less thug on the streets.
- Minus any LP lost to escalations (the bank can't go below 0).

Then play Part 2 as written, starting from that total.

### Fort Drelev's defenses

Use Appendix 3's fortification statistics:

| Fortification | Stats | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Drelev Keep** (B2) | Keep: AC 25, HP 5, up to 4 armies | The Fort Guard garrisons here. |
| **Watchtower** (B1) | Tower: AC 20, HP 2, 1 army | |
| **Town wall** | **Before Clock 16:** the gap makes it no better than a trench (AC 15, HP 1, 1 army). **From Clock 16:** Wooden Wall (AC 15, HP 2, 2 armies). | |

Fortified armies can't be engaged. To dig them out you need **siege engines**: a
mustered Siege army, the Stonethrowers or the Siege Battery turned with *Turn Their
Coin*, or the PCs opening the way from inside.

### Three ways in

**1. Infiltrate (the module's way).** The armies stay out of town or besiege it. The
PCs use p. 303's infiltration tactics, the secret tunnel if Satinder showed it
(Mission 8), and the banked LP. Each enemy army the campaign destroyed means fewer
patrols. If the **Fort Guard army was destroyed**, street patrols appear only half as
often (double the hours each infiltration tactic earns).

**2. Siege (starve them out).** One of your non-skirmisher armies takes the **Besiege**
order in the Fort Drelev hex.
- Each Night of siege, every enemy Mercenary army inside gains 1 Doubt, as unpaid
  sell-swords start slipping away. The town also suffers: apply the module's
  *Languishing* penalty (−2 LP per Night, to a minimum of 0).
- Drelev doesn't break from a siege alone. The PCs still have to go in after him, but
  with fewer defenders left.

**3. Storm (the Battle of Fort Drelev).** A **named battle**: a full War encounter
against every enemy army in the hex, with the fortifications above. **Strike the
Commander** means the PCs go into Drelev Keep after the baron while the armies fight
in the streets. Run the keep (areas C1–C51) as the PCs' half of the battle; the
**escape tunnel** puts them straight into the dungeons.
- **Victory:** Drelev's armies in town break. Bank +6 LP (+8 on a critical success
  when Determining Victory), even past the usual cap of 10. Street patrols end, so the
  PCs can move freely through the town.
- **Collateral:** each fortification your siege engines destroyed costs 2 LP (fires
  in the streets), applied as *Public Destruction*.
- **Loss:** your armies are routed from the hex, and the PCs inside the keep are on
  their own.

## 9. Ending the War

### Victory: Fort Drelev liberated
- Follow **Claiming Drelev** (p. 317). Drelev's hexes become unclaimed and can be
  annexed, and Fort Drelev becomes a freehold.
- **Campaign award:** 120 Kingdom XP (banked), Unrest −1d4, and one Ruin of the
  players' choice −1. If Strain ended **below 5**, also gain 1 Fame.
- Run the **Reckoning** (`rules.md` §11).

### Terms (Daily Event 17, or the PCs propose them)
At a parley, Drelev offers peace: the PCs **recognize his barony** and **cede every
Contested and enemy-held hex**, and he returns any prisoners.
- **Accept:** the war ends. Run the Reckoning, and every Contested hex is lost without
  a check. Fort Drelev can still be infiltrated later as in Part 2, but every escalation
  stays in force. Expect the PCs' honor and Kisandra's trust to suffer.
- **Stall:** a Statecraft check (Control DC) drags out the talks. On a success, War
  Clock −1. On a critical success, also reveal one enemy army. On a critical failure,
  the envoy storms off, and War Clock +2.
- **Counter-demand surrender:** Drelev refuses unless 3 or more of his armies are
  already destroyed. If they are, he flees to his keep, and the PCs can go straight to
  the Fall of Fort Drelev with War Clock −3.

### Armed Truce (War Clock 30)
Irovetti guarantees Drelev's throne. The war ends without a decision. Run the
Reckoning. Every Contested hex gets the normal check, and enemy-held hexes stay lost.
Drelev remains in power with every escalation in force, and Pitax is openly hostile.
That's a strong lead into Chapter 8.

### Collapse (Strain 20)
The kingdom's will breaks and the armies come home. Treat this as **Terms accepted**,
and the Collapse of Will crisis still applies.

## 10. Pacing Notes

- **Expected length:** 15–25 days. The War Clock alone reaches 30 by Day 30, and a
  good campaign pushes it back several times.
- **Day 1 should feel urgent:** raiders are loose, the bridge is held against you,
  and the Outriders arrive at Dusk on Day 2. Give the players a clear first decision:
  protect the farms, take the bridge, or go for the camp?
- **Rushing the fort is allowed but costly.** A party that goes straight for Fort
  Drelev on Day 3 faces every garrison, no banked LP, and raiders burning the
  Narlmarches behind them. That's a legitimate choice. Make the consequences visible.
- **Armies hold, heroes act.** The most memorable days will be the ones where the
  party leaves its levies dug in at Tatzlford to hunt Speartooth or burn the camp, and
  rides back to find out whether the line held.
